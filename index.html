Peace of mind

Will it take forever?

Your cabinets are built in our own Oldsmar shop, not ordered from a cabinet supplier. Production takes up to 6 weeks after you approve the design.

Will the price creep up?

You get an itemized quote before anything is built. The price changes only if you change the order, and we agree on any change with you before production.

What if the company disappears mid-project?

PalmCraft holds all required licenses and is general liability insured, and you can visit our workshop by appointment. You pay in stages as the work moves forward, and the last 20% is due only after installation and the final punch list.

Will they actually listen to me?

Our designer walks you through every choice and shows it in 3D. Two rounds of design changes are included, and we work alongside your own designer if you have one.

Will I be able to reach anyone?

During business hours we call back within minutes. Every project gets its own WhatsApp group, so you always know where your cabinets are.

Will the rooms match each other?

Kitchen, TV wall, closets, and baths are designed as one system by one team, so finishes and details match across the whole house.

How it works

  1. Request & Consultation. Send the short form or call. We discuss your room, style, and budget, and we reply within 2 hours during business hours. The estimate is free and carries no obligation.
  2. Measurements & Design. We measure your space for free, review the layout, and prepare a free 3D design with materials, colors, and hardware for your approval.
  3. Production. After final design approval and the first deposit, production takes up to 6 weeks. Cabinets are ready for your inspection before delivery.
  4. Delivery & Installation. PalmCraft delivers and professionally installs its own cabinetry across Tampa Bay, finishing with a hardware check and cleanup.

Our pricing: custom kitchens start from $10,000 for a 12-linear-foot kitchen with design, delivery, and installation.

As the work moves forward — the last payment only when your kitchen is finished: 50% deposit, 30% cabinets built, 15% installed, 5% punch list done.
